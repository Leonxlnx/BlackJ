JUnit libraries bundled with BlackJ
===================================

These jars are unmodified copies from Maven Central, downloaded and SHA-1 checked by
scripts/fetch-junit.cjs. BlackJ uses them to compile and run the students' test classes.

  Project: https://junit.org/junit5/
  This jar also contains (shaded) opentest4j 1.3.0 and apiguardian-api 1.1.2 (Apache-2.0),
  picocli and univocity-parsers (Apache-2.0), JUnit 4.13.2 (EPL-1.0) and Hamcrest Core 1.3
  (BSD-3-Clause). Their licence texts are inside the jar under META-INF/.
